📐 By Sides

Triangles can be classified by comparing the lengths of their three sides.

Equilateral

All three sides are equal. All three angles are exactly 60°. The most symmetrical triangle!

Isosceles

Exactly two sides are equal. The angles opposite the equal sides are also equal.

Scalene

All three sides have different lengths. All three angles are different too.

📐 By Angles

Triangles can also be classified by the size of their largest angle.

Acute

All angles are less than 90°. Every equilateral triangle is acute.

Right

Has exactly one 90° angle. The side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse — the longest side.

Obtuse

Has exactly one angle greater than 90°. The other two must be acute.

🔺 Angle Sum Property

The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is always 180°. No matter how you stretch or squish a triangle, this never changes!

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

🔄 Exterior Angle Theorem

An exterior angle of a triangle is formed when one side is extended. The exterior angle equals the sum of the two non-adjacent (remote) interior angles.

✨ Special Triangle Properties

Equilateral

  • ▸ All sides equal
  • ▸ All angles = 60°
  • ▸ 3 lines of symmetry
  • ▸ Always acute

Isosceles

  • ▸ Two sides equal
  • ▸ Base angles equal
  • ▸ 1 line of symmetry
  • ▸ Can be acute, right, or obtuse

Scalene

  • ▸ All sides different
  • ▸ All angles different
  • ▸ No lines of symmetry
  • ▸ Can be acute, right, or obtuse

🌍 Why Triangles Matter

Triangles are everywhere — from ancient pyramids to modern skyscrapers. They are the strongest shape in nature because their rigid structure can't be deformed without changing a side length. Here's where they show up:

🌉

Bridges & Buildings

Truss bridges use networks of triangles to distribute weight. The triangular shape prevents the structure from collapsing under heavy loads.

🏔️

Surveying & Navigation

Triangulation is used by GPS satellites and surveyors to pinpoint exact locations by measuring angles from known points.

🎮

Computer Graphics

Every 3D model in video games is made of thousands of tiny triangles (called a mesh). Triangles are the simplest polygon that defines a flat surface.

✈️

Aviation & Aerospace

Delta (triangular) wings on fighter jets reduce drag at supersonic speeds. Pilots use triangle-based trigonometry for navigation.

🏗️

Roofs & Architecture

Sloped roofs are triangular so rain and snow slide off. The A-frame design provides maximum strength with minimal material.

🎵

Music & Art

The triangle instrument, the "rule of thirds" in photography, and the golden triangle in design all use this fundamental shape.

💡 Fun Facts

  • The Great Pyramid of Giza has four triangular faces and has stood for over 4,500 years — proof of triangular strength!
  • The Bermuda Triangle isn't really a triangle — it's a loosely defined region, but the name stuck because triangles are so recognizable.
  • Geodesic domes (like the Epcot ball) are built entirely from triangles — they enclose the most volume with the least surface area.
  • Your smartphone's touchscreen uses a mesh of triangles to detect exactly where your finger is.
