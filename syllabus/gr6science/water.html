Section 3.7 — AI Integrated Lesson Plan

Water

Science, Class 6, NCERT • Chapter 14
Semantris AI for Sustainability 2–3 periods of 40 min
📚 Subject & AI Integration

Content: Importance of water — students will be guided on how AI is helping in water sustainability.

AI Tools:

🤖 Semantris 🌎 AI for Sustainability
🎯 Learning Objectives

The students will be able to:

  • Understand the importance of water.
  • Become aware about the places facing scarcity of water.
  • Understand water pollution and the threat to marine life.
  • Become aware about the role of technology in water sustainability.
🏫 Classroom Arrangement

Regular classroom setup having projector, computer/laptop and internet.

📝 Material Required
  • Day 1: Paper and pen.
  • Day 2: Computer and internet.
✅ Pre-Preparation Activities

📅 Day 1 — Individual Activity:

To estimate the use of water by the student and his/her family. Students will collect the data on the amount of water used by one person in a day for various activities like:

  1. Washing hands
  2. Bathing
  3. Flushing
  4. Drinking
  5. Any other

📅 Day 2 — Group Activity: CASE STUDY

Divide the class into two groups A and B.

  • Group A will work on a case study on a city/village in India facing water scarcity.
  • Group B will work on a city outside India facing acute water shortage.
💡 Previous Knowledge
  • For what all activities do we use water?
  • How is water useful to us?
  • Is water adequately available everywhere to all living organisms?
  • Name the animals that migrate from one place to another in search of water.
🛠 Methodology

📅 Day 1:

  • Students will be taken to computer labs and asked to play the game 🎲 Semantris individually and write the words which are related to water.
    🔗 Semantris — research.google.com/semantris
  • Based on data collected by students regarding usage of water at home by every individual, the teacher will take the name of one student who consumed most water and the one who consumed less amount of water. The teacher will ask the reason behind it.
  • After collecting the information from all students, the topic “WATER” will be introduced. The teacher will also cover:
    • Discuss the importance of water.
    • How can water be used in a limited amount.
    • Define water pollution.
    • 🎥 Showing the video AI for Good – Sustainability.

📅 Day 2:

  • After collecting the information, students will be asked to present their case study. Other groups will be taking notes.
  • After the discussion, students will be taken to a computer lab and asked to visit the AI website:
    🌊 Diving into an Acidifying Ocean — artsexperiments.withgoogle.com
  • Students will further explore on YouTube how AI is playing a major role in Sustainability.
  • 🎥 Showing them the video Countdown to Day Zero: Cape Town’s Water Crisis.
  • Students can explore the following videos:
    🎥 AquaGen AI & IoT Based Water Management System
    🎥 World Cities Day: AI empowers urban wastewater treatment in China
    🎥 AI & Water Sustainability
🎓 Learning Outcomes

Learners will be able to understand the importance of water and the need to save water.

🔍 Follow up Activities
  • Ask students about steps taken to save water on an individual level.
  • 🎲 Fun water game — WATERTYPE:
    🔗 Water Type — experiments.withgoogle.com/water-type
💭 Reflections

Importance of conserving water and AI steps towards sustainability.

📖 Glossary

AI Related Terminologies

Diving into an Acidifying Ocean

It is an interactive data visualisation, inviting you to dive into the ocean and explore the impact of rising temperatures on marine life.

🌊 Diving into an Acidifying Ocean — artsexperiments.withgoogle.com

Semantris

A Google experiment, Semantris is a word association game powered by machine learning. Each time you enter a word that is associated with the target word, the AI looks at all the words in play and chooses the ones it thinks are most related.

🎲 Semantris — research.google.com/semantris
