AI Course Overview

AI Curriculum & Readiness

Developing a deep readiness for understanding and appreciating Artificial Intelligence and its application in our lives.

Course Objectives

01. Immersion

Understanding the world of AI through games, activities, and multisensorial learning to become AI-Ready.

02. Three Domains

Introducing the three core domains of AI (Data, CV, NLP) in an age-appropriate manner.

03. Hands-On Learning

Constructing meaning through interactive participation and engaging activities.

04. The Project Cycle

Learning the structural framework of how AI projects are built from start to finish.

05. Python Skills

Introduction to programming skills using basic Python coding language.

Learning Outcomes

Foundations & Society

  • Identify Artificial Intelligence and describe its applications in daily life.
  • Relate and reflect on Human-Machine Interactions.
  • Imagine and examine skills required for futuristic job opportunities.
  • Understand the impact of AI on Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
  • Gain awareness of AI bias, access issues, and ethical considerations.
  • Develop effective communication and collaborative work skills.
  • Unleash imagination regarding smart homes and interactive storytelling.

The AI Project Cycle

  • Identify the AI Project framework and learn "Problem Scoping."
  • Understand the iterative nature of scoping and goal setting.
  • Brainstorm ethical issues surrounding selected problems.
  • Identify data requirements and find reliable sources.
  • Use various graphs to visualize acquired data.

Technical Mastery & Coding

  • Domains of AI: Interact with Data, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing.
  • Decision Trees: Understand, create, and implement decision logic.
  • Computer Vision: Visualize the computer’s ability to identify alphabets and handwriting.
  • Neural Networks: Appreciate the concept through gamification.
  • Python: Acquire introductory programming skills in a user-friendly format.
