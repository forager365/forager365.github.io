Course Outline

AI Learning Journey

Session Plan & Learning Outcomes
1
Excite
▼
Session

Introduction to AI

Setting up the context of the curriculum and introducing the fundamentals.

Learning Outcome

To identify and appreciate Artificial Intelligence and describe its applications in daily life.

Ice Breaker Activity

Dream Smart Home

Learners design a rough layout or floor plan of their dream smart home.

Learning Outcome

Creative application of AI concepts to visualize daily life enhancements.

Recommended Activity

The AI Games

Learners participate in games based on different AI domains:

  • ✂️ Rock, Paper, Scissors: Data Domain
  • 🔮 Mystery Animal: NLP Domain
  • 📸 Emoji Scavenger Hunt: Computer Vision Domain
Learning Outcome

To relate, apply and reflect on Human-Machine Interactions. To identify and interact with the three domains of AI.

Assessment

AI Quiz

Paper-Pen or Online Quiz.

Learning Outcome

To undergo an assessment for analyzing progress towards acquired AI-Readiness skills.

Activity

Letter to Future Self

Writing a letter to one's self keeping the future in context. Describing what they have learnt or hope to learn.

Learning Outcome

To imagine, examine and reflect on the skills required for futuristic job opportunities.

2
Relate
▼
Video Session

Smart Cities & Homes

Video introducing the concept of Smart Cities, Smart Schools, and Smart Homes.

Learning Outcome

Learners to relate to the application of Artificial Intelligence in their daily lives.

Activity

Write an Interactive Story

Draw a floor plan of a Home/School/City and write an interactive story around it using the Story Speaker extension.

Learning Outcome

To unleash imagination towards smart homes, build interactive stories, and reflect on Human-Machine Interactions.

3
Purpose
▼
Session

Sustainable Development Goals

Introduction to SDGs and their relationship with AI.

Learning Outcome

To understand the impact of AI on Sustainable Development Goals to develop responsible citizenship.

Activity

Go Goals Board Game

Learners answer questions specifically related to Sustainable Development Goals.

Learning Outcome

Reinforcement of SDG concepts through gamified learning.

4
Possibilities
▼
Research Session

Theme-based Case Studies

Listening to case studies of inspiring startups/companies using AI. Learners research present trends and visualize the future of their allotted theme.

Learning Outcome

To research and develop awareness of skills required for jobs of the future.

Activity

Job Ad Creation

Create a job advertisement describing the nature of a job and skillset required 10 years down the line, predicting how AI will transform it.

Learning Outcome

To develop effective communication and collaborative work skills while reflecting on future opportunities.

5
AI Ethics
▼
Video Session

Discussing about AI Ethics

Watching a video and discussing key ethical concepts in Artificial Intelligence.

Learning Outcome

To understand and reflect on the ethical issues around AI.

Recommended Activity

Ethics Awareness

Role-playing activity where students act as major stakeholders to decide what is ethical/unethical in a given scenario.

Learning Outcome

To understand and reflect on the ethical issues around AI.

Session

AI Bias and AI Access

  • Discussing possible bias in data collection.
  • Discussing the implications of AI technology.
Learning Outcome

To gain awareness around AI bias and AI access.

Recommended Activity

Balloon Debate

Students divide into teams (Affirmative vs. Against) on the same theme to debate why AI is beneficial or harmful to society.

Learning Outcome

To let the students analyze the advantages and disadvantages of Artificial Intelligence.
