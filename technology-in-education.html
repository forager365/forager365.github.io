c. 370 BCE · Writing
They will cease to exercise memory because they rely on that which is written, calling things to remembrance no longer from within themselves, but by means of external marks. — Plato, Phaedrus

Every learning technology begins with the fear that it will replace thinking. Writing was the first.

c. 1440 · Printing

Movable type

In Germany, around 1440, the goldsmith Johannes Gutenberg invents the movable-type printing press.

  • Societal and economic transformation
  • Spread of new ideas
  • Democratization of knowledge
  • Rural farming communities also benefited from published almanacs
1 in 100 → 20 in 100 Only 1 in 100 people was educated in the year 1400. It took 400–500 years to reach 20 in 100.

The pattern · Offloading

Technology changed learning

Each tool takes over a routine mental chore — and frees attention for something harder.

Spell check

Reduced the need to memorize every spelling.

Freed up → expression

Contact lists

We no longer need to remember phone numbers.

Freed up → memory

Calculators

Help students focus on concepts, reasoning, and problem-solving.

Freed up → reasoning

Socrates feared writing would weaken memory. He was half right — every tool since has moved memory outward, and moved learning upward.

Now · Artificial Intelligence

The next major step

AI continues the same shift — taking over routine work, and changing what students must practice.

AI can help students

  • Generate ideas
  • Explain difficult concepts
  • Translate languages
  • Check writing
  • Practice problems
  • Receive feedback more quickly
the focus
shifts

Students must now learn to

  • Ask good questions
  • Evaluate information
  • Think critically
  • Solve problems
  • Use technology responsibly

This does not mean students no longer need to learn — it changes what learning means.
